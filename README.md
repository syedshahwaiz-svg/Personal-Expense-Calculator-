# Personal-Expense-Calculator-
Keeps your daily life expenses at one place and let's you know when you are out of budget 

Benefits:
-keeps you up-to-date 
-Calculates big to small every expence
-helps you safe money 
-time saver especially for small business owners 

Future improvements:

-add date to remember the day of expence
-automatically notify you when you are getting low on Budget 
-use Ai to help you to save money
